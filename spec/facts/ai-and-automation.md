# AI and Automation

Shay does not just manage AI vendors. He designs, builds, and ships production AI systems himself. The examples below are business-oriented outcomes, not code walkthroughs.

## Enterprise support agent
An AI Tier-0 support channel for 11,000 employees, integrated with knowledge, incident, request, and escalation workflows. It reduced relevant Service Desk workload by ~35%. See case-studies.md for full detail.

## Continuous compliance agents
AI-driven continuous audit support covering ISO 27001, SOC 2, and GDPR. It freed roughly 3 FTEs of repetitive manual audit effort and improved the consistency and breadth of audit coverage.

## Sales SKU agent
An agent that guides salespeople through compatible product combinations across hundreds of SKUs, reducing dependency on tribal knowledge.

## Self-managed Instagram content studio
A production-style platform with eight orchestrated AI agents operating as a marketing department:
- CMO and orchestrator
- Reels strategy
- Daily stories
- Sales stories
- Long-form content
- Performance analytics
- A/B experimentation
- Knowledge librarian

It uses RAG-style versioned brand and audience context, cost and usage accounting, an admin dashboard, and Instagram analytics integration.

## Multi-channel AI assistant
A WhatsApp and Telegram assistant spanning customer-facing and private executive-assistant agents. It integrates with CRM, booking, Google Calendar, and Gmail. The tool-using agents handle lead intake, booking, reminders, follow-ups, and daily briefings.
