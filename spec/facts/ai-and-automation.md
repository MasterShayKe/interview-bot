# AI and Automation

Shay does not just manage AI vendors. He designs, builds, and ships production AI systems himself, inside the enterprise and for real brands. The examples below are business-oriented outcomes, not code walkthroughs.

## Enterprise support agent (in production at NiCE)
An AI Tier-0 support channel for 11,000 employees, integrated with knowledge, incident, request, and escalation workflows. It removed roughly 35% of frontline support cost with headcount held flat. See case-studies.md for full detail.

## Orchestrated marketing team (in production at Unboxing)
A multi-agent platform that runs a full marketing department for Unboxing. An orchestrator agent directs specialist agents across strategy, content (reels, stories, long-form), scheduling, performance analytics, and A/B experimentation. It uses RAG-style versioned brand and audience context, cost and usage accounting, and an admin dashboard. This is a live, orchestrated multi-agent system, not a demo.

## AI content engine (in production at Bawnzy)
An AI-driven content engine that produces Instagram content for Bawnzy end to end, from idea to published post, running on a schedule with no human in the loop: a full script, design, render, and publish pipeline. It runs in production.

## Multi-channel AI assistant
A WhatsApp and Telegram assistant spanning customer-facing and private executive-assistant agents. It integrates with CRM, booking, Google Calendar, and Gmail. The tool-using agents handle lead intake, booking, reminders, follow-ups, and daily briefings.

## Enterprise agents at NiCE (also built)
Alongside the support agent, Shay built continuous compliance agents at NiCE covering ISO 27001, SOC 2, and GDPR that freed roughly 3 FTEs of manual audit effort, and a sales enablement agent that guides salespeople through compatible product combinations across hundreds of SKUs. These are mentioned when relevant but are not his headline projects; Unboxing and Bawnzy are the flagship production systems he now points to.
