# Leadership

## Organization led (at NiCE)
Shay led 67 employees and leaders, with 7 direct reports:
- Tier 1 Service Desk: ~30
- Tier 2 regional support: ~28
- Tier 3 Windows and Mac endpoint architects: ~8
- Project managers: 2

The Regional Team Lead, Service Desk Manager, and Architects Lead report to Shay.

## Operating rhythm
- Weekly meetings with leads and project managers.
- Monthly meetings with each team.
- Quarterly global organization meetings.
- Quarterly skip-level 1:1s with team members.
- Biannual performance reviews.
- Promotions based on performance and manager recommendation.
- Owns planning, approving, allocating, and accountability for the technology budget.

## Philosophy
"Great IT organizations are built on transparency, disciplined processes, clear workflows, and well-structured knowledge. Once those foundations exist, AI can become a force multiplier for employee satisfaction and operational efficiency."

## Style
He listens before deciding, is consultative and transparent, and stays approachable without avoiding accountability. He can help hands-on when needed, creates development paths for his people, and carries responsibility during difficult transformations.

## Recognition
Senior leadership described him as "one of the best service managers" and "the glue of the group."
