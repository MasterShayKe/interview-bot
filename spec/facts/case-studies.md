# Case Studies

Each case study follows Challenge / Action / Outcome. Metrics are from NiCE unless noted.

## 1. Modern endpoint provisioning
- **Challenge:** Laptop provisioning took ~4-5 hours and required the corporate office network.
- **Action:** Led the move from SCCM and local provisioning to Microsoft Intune and Windows Autopilot.
- **Outcome:** Provisioning dropped to ~2 hours max, and devices became provisionable anywhere with an internet connection. Covered ~3,000 laptops per year and saved ~9,000 technician hours per year. As repetitive provisioning declined, regional technicians could progress into endpoint architecture roles.

## 2. AI-powered Tier-0 support
- **Challenge:** High volume of recurring questions and manual ticket handling.
- **Action:** Designed and deployed an AI support channel for the entire NiCE workforce. It can walk users through approved knowledge base articles, recommend troubleshooting, open service requests and incidents on the user's behalf, check and escalate existing tickets, and transfer physical, lifecycle, onboarding, offboarding, and hands-on issues that fall outside automation scope.
- **Outcome:** Used across an 11,000-employee environment. Reduced relevant Service Desk workload by ~35% and automated hundreds to ~1,000 interactions depending on volume and period.

## 3. Global Live Chat
- **Challenge:** Employees relied on conventional ticket channels.
- **Action:** Introduced the company's first support Live Chat, secured CIO approval, piloted it, and rolled it out globally.
- **Outcome:** Handled ~150-200 chats per day and helped raise First Contact Resolution from 65% to 80%.

## 4. Zero-disruption M&A integration
- **Challenge:** Acquired organizations needed fast integration into corporate identity, endpoint, infrastructure, and support standards.
- **Action:** Created and iteratively improved a structured M&A integration playbook.
- **Outcome:** Delivered 8 acquisitions of ~50-400 employees each, primarily European, with typical end-to-end integration in 2-3 months and zero operational downtime or disruption.

## 5. Contractor security transformation
- **Challenge:** Contractors used unmanaged personal devices on the corporate network.
- **Action:** Moved contractors from BYOD to fully managed corporate devices and a controlled access methodology.
- **Outcome:** ~800 were initially planned, and nearly 2,000 contractors were migrated in the first year, improving governance, endpoint control, and corporate access security.

## 6. Enterprise Mac adoption
- **Challenge:** Macs were available only to a few UX designers.
- **Action:** Tested MacBooks with individual business units and measured developer workflow performance before expanding support.
- **Outcome:** Introduced MacBooks to development populations. Testing showed ~70% faster performance for relevant workflows, and he built enterprise Mac support and management capabilities.
