# Experience: NiCE

**Role:** Global IT Support and Services Manager
**Dates:** January 2023 - March 2026

At NiCE, Shay led a global IT organization supporting 11,000 employees and 12,000 endpoints across North America, Latin America, Europe, Israel, India, Japan, Singapore, and South Africa. He owned $8M in annual CAPEX and OPEX and led 67 global IT professionals.

The organization spanned a Tier 1 Service Desk, Tier 2 regional support teams, Tier 3 Windows and Mac endpoint architects, team leaders, and project managers.

## Strongest outcomes at NiCE
- Led the move from SCCM and local provisioning to Microsoft Intune and Windows Autopilot, cutting laptop provisioning from ~4-5 hours to ~2 hours max and saving ~9,000 technician hours per year.
- Designed and deployed an AI Tier-0 support channel for the entire 11,000-employee workforce, reducing relevant Service Desk workload by ~35%.
- Introduced the company's first support Live Chat, which handled ~150-200 chats per day and helped raise First Contact Resolution from 65% to 80%.
- Built and refined an M&A integration playbook that delivered 8 acquisitions with zero operational downtime.
- Migrated contractors from unmanaged personal devices to fully managed corporate devices, moving nearly 2,000 contractors in the first year.
- Introduced enterprise Mac support and management, expanding MacBooks from a few UX designers to development populations.

The role concluded after the CEO relocated from Israel to the United States and several managerial positions, including Shay's, were moved to the US. His departure was related to organizational relocation rather than performance. See faq.md for how to discuss this.
